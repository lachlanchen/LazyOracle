## Reviewer walkthrough

No login, subscription, birth profile, camera permission or network is needed
for this walkthrough.

1. Open **Change Atlas** on the home screen. Select **1 · The Creative**.
2. Tap any of its six lines. The before/after construction, resulting hexagram,
   and upper/lower trigrams update from deterministic local rules. Flip all six
   lines to obtain **2 · The Receptive**. Reset to repeat the experiment.
3. Explore the nuclear, opposite and inverse transformations; each opens the
   corresponding figure for further deliberate exploration. Expand the Chinese
   source text below a figure to inspect the classical judgement.
4. Tap **Save to notebook**. Open **Reading notebook** from home and select the
   saved entry. Its original calculation is preserved separately from notes.
5. Record a reflection, a small action and (later) an observation. Tap **Save
   notes**. Relaunch: both the original calculation and the notes remain.
6. Mark the entry reviewed, filter pending entries, or explicitly export a text
   copy using the system share sheet. Existing practices can also save their
   computed results to the same notebook without rerunning them.

## How this changes the experience

The home screen now leads with an interactive study tool and a local reflection
workflow. Change Atlas lets users construct and compare all 64 hexagrams without
asking a prediction question, casting randomly or requesting generated text.
The notebook connects an exact result to the user's own action and observation;
it does not present model-generated prose as the underlying calculation.
Both features work offline and use native SwiftUI / Compose controls. Existing
traditional practices remain available below these features.

This is not a claim that no other app includes I Ching study or journaling.
Apple must still assess whether the complete experience meets 4.3(b).

Name: LazyOracle: Atlas & Notebook

Promotional text:
Explore the 64 hexagrams by changing individual lines. Save exact results in a
private notebook, add a small action, and return to record what you observed.

Description:

LazyOracle is a native space for exploring traditional symbolic systems and
recording your own reflections.

CHANGE ATLAS
Choose any of the 64 I Ching hexagrams. Change individual lines, compare the
before and after figures, inspect their upper and lower trigrams, and explore
nuclear, opposite and inverse transformations. Read a concise explanation or
expand the classical Chinese source. Every transformation is calculated locally;
this study tool needs neither random casting nor an AI response.

A NOTEBOOK THAT KEEPS THE ORIGINAL
Save an exact calculated result from the Atlas or an existing practice. Add your
own reflection, one small action and a later observation. Mark entries reviewed,
filter those still pending, and export a text copy when you choose. Editing a
note never changes the original calculation.

TRADITIONAL PRACTICES
Explore I Ching casting, Tarot, BaZi, astrology, the Chinese almanac, Eight
Mansions feng shui, face and palm forms, and the Books of Answers and Questions.
Native live face and palm views analyse landmarks on your device. Interpretations
are offered as cultural and symbolic perspectives for reflection and entertainment.

OFFLINE RULES, OPTIONAL ONLINE EXPLANATIONS
The Atlas, notebook and deterministic calculations work offline. Optional Tianji
explanations and chat use an online AI service. Asking for an explanation sends
the selected reading and your question to that service; it does not redraw or
recast your result. No account is required. Notebook entries and notes are saved
locally; sharing a notebook entry is an explicit action.

Available in English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean,
Vietnamese, Spanish, French, German, Russian and Arabic.

LazyOracle does not predict events or provide professional advice.
