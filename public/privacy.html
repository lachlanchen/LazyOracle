LazyOracle Privacy Policy

Updated 29 September 2026 · LazyingArt LLC

LazyOracle computes Tarot draws, BaZi (四柱八字) charts, I Ching casts, natal charts, feng shui sectors, palm and face readings, and Book of Answers pages on your device. This policy explains exactly what the app stores and what, if anything, leaves your device.

What we collect

LazyOracle has no account system and no analytics. Our relay does not retain the content of your questions, birth details or readings.

Optional Tianji explanations and chat. When you ask Tianji for an explanation or send a chat message, the app sends your question, relevant conversation history or its summary, and the structured facts the engines computed through our relay at oracle.lazying.art or oracle-fast.lazying.art to an online language-model provider. These facts may include birth details supplied for a chart or in chat, and measured hand or face features. Camera images are not uploaded. The relay forwards the content without retaining request content; it records operational counts and statuses. The provider processes the transmitted content to generate the answer. Avoid including information you do not want processed online.

The native app's calculations, Change Atlas and reading notebook do not need Tianji. To use them entirely offline, do not request an explanation or send chat messages. The classic web interface also has a Tianji Cloud switch in Settings. An on-device language-model download is not required by the current native app.

What stays on your device

  • Birth details (date, time, sex at birth, birthplace) that you enter for BaZi, astrology and feng shui are saved in the app's local storage. Details included in a requested online explanation or chat are also sent with that request.
  • Interface language and model settings.
  • Saved practice state, conversations and reading notebook. The native app saves results and conversations locally so you can return to them. Notebook entries keep the original calculated facts separately from your reflection, action, observation and reviewed status. Notebook notes are not automatically sent to Tianji. Exporting an entry opens the system share sheet; your chosen destination then receives that copy. There is no account-based notebook sync. Device backups may contain local app data according to your device settings.
  • Palm and face camera images are not stored. The native live views process camera frames in memory to measure landmarks. The classic interface measures a selected photo in memory. Images are not uploaded, and no face is recognised or matched against an identity. Computed measurements may remain in saved practice results or notebook entries.

Network use

  • Loading the web app fetches its files from oracle.lazying.art or its mirror. The native app bundles its interface and calculation rules.
  • Tianji explanations and chat send content as described above when requested.
  • Camera and motion sensors are used on the device for face/hand landmarks and compass heading.

Children

LazyOracle is not directed at children under 13. Do not include children's personal information in online questions or chat.

Entertainment purposes

Readings are generated for reflection and entertainment. They are not medical, legal, financial or safety advice and do not predict events.
