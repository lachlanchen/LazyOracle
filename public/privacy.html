LazyOracle Privacy Policy

Effective 21 September 2026 · LazyingArt LLC

LazyOracle computes Tarot draws, BaZi (四柱八字) charts, I Ching casts, natal charts, feng shui sectors, palm readings and Book of Answers pages on your device. This policy explains exactly what the app stores and what, if anything, leaves your device.

What we collect

LazyOracle has no account system and no analytics. LazyingArt LLC does not store your questions, your birth details, your photos or your readings.

Tianji Cloud (off by default). If you switch it on in Settings and no on-device model is downloaded, the narrative reading is written by a language model through our reading relay at oracle.lazying.art. The app sends only the structured facts of the reading (for example the cards drawn, or the four pillars) and your question; the relay forwards them to the model provider and does not log or retain the content. While it is off, nothing leaves the device.

What stays on your device

  • Birth details (date, time, sex at birth, birthplace) that you enter for BaZi, astrology and feng shui. They are stored in the app's local storage and can be deleted at any time from Settings.
  • Interface language and model settings.
  • A downloaded language model, if you choose one in Settings. It is cached by the app so readings work offline.
  • Palm photos are not stored. A photo is analysed in memory on the device to find the hand outline and is discarded when you leave the screen.

Network use

  • Downloading an on-device model (optional) fetches a file from oracle.lazying.art. The request contains no personal data.
  • Tianji Cloud as described above, only while it is switched on.
  • Camera and motion sensors are used only on the device for the palm photo and the compass heading.

Children

LazyOracle is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Entertainment purposes

Readings are generated for reflection and entertainment. They are not medical, legal, financial or safety advice and do not predict events.
