LazyOracle Support

LazyOracle is made by LazyingArt LLC. Charts are computed on your device, with cloud narration available through Tianji Cloud. There is no account to recover.

Common questions

Readings say they were "composed from the computed facts". How do I get a narrative reading?

Tianji Cloud is enabled by default and needs an internet connection. If it is off, enable it in Settings. If the service is unavailable, individual practices still compose readings from the facts on your device. No language-model download is needed.

The compass does not move.

On iPhone, tap "Enable the compass" and allow motion access. Hold the phone flat and away from magnets. Some tablets and desktop browsers have no compass; read the sectors from a map instead.

No hand is found in my palm photo.

Fill the frame with one open palm in good light, fingers slightly spread, and avoid strong shadows.

My birth time is unknown.

Tick "I do not know the birth time". The hour pillar and the Ascendant are then approximate and the app says so.

Can I delete my birth details?

Yes: Settings → "Delete birth details from this device".
