LazyOracle Privacy Policy

Effective 21 September 2026 · LazyingArt LLC

LazyOracle computes Tarot draws, BaZi (四柱八字) charts, I Ching casts, natal charts, feng shui sectors, palm and face readings, and Book of Answers pages on your device. This policy explains exactly what the app stores and what, if anything, leaves your device.

What we collect

LazyOracle has no account system and no analytics. LazyingArt LLC does not store your questions, your birth details, your photos or your readings.

Tianji Cloud (on by default, and switchable off). The reading written in words is composed by a language model through our own relay at oracle.lazying.art or oracle-fast.lazying.art, which holds the provider key so the app does not have to. The app sends your question, relevant conversation history or its summary, and the structured facts the engines computed. These may include birth details supplied for a chart or in chat, and measured hand or face features. Photos are not uploaded. The relay forwards this content to the model provider without retaining request content. Switch it off in Settings and the app composes the reading itself, on the device, and no cloud reading requests are sent.

What stays on your device

  • Birth details (date, time, sex at birth, birthplace) that you enter for BaZi, astrology and feng shui. They are saved in the app's local storage and can be deleted at any time from Settings. Details included in a cloud reading or chat are also sent with that request.
  • Interface language and model settings.
  • Palm and face photos are not stored. A photo is measured in memory on the device, to find the hand outline or the points of the face, and is discarded when you leave the screen. No photo is uploaded, and no face is recognised or matched against anything.

Network use

  • Loading the app fetches it from oracle.lazying.art. The request contains no personal data.
  • Tianji Cloud as described above, only while it is switched on.
  • Camera and motion sensors are used only on the device, for the palm or face photo and for the compass heading.

Children

LazyOracle is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Entertainment purposes

Readings are generated for reflection and entertainment. They are not medical, legal, financial or safety advice and do not predict events.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Questions: echomind@lazying.art, LazyingArt LLC.
