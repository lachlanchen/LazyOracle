### Full description

Eight ways to ask a question, one quiet place to ask it.

LazyOracle calculates every reading on your phone. The cards are shuffled here, the four pillars and the natal chart are computed here, the hexagram is cast here. A small language model can run on the device too, so a full narrative reading never needs a network.

WHAT IS INSIDE
• Tarot: 78 cards, one-card, three-card and Celtic Cross spreads, upright and reversed meanings
• BaZi 四柱八字: four pillars with true solar time, hidden stems, ten gods, five-element balance, luck cycles and the current year
• I Ching 周易: three-coin or yarrow casting, the classical judgement, changing lines and the resulting hexagram
• Astrology: tropical natal chart with whole-sign houses, aspects and today's transits
• Feng Shui 八宅: your personal trigram and the eight sectors of your home on a live compass
• Palmistry: hand shape from a photo analysed on the device, plus your three major lines
• Book of Answers and Book of Questions: original pages to open when you need one line

HOW READINGS WORK
Every practice works with no model at all: the app composes a reading from the computed facts. For a narrative reading, download an on-device model once in Settings, or point the app at your own server.

PRIVACY
No account. No analytics. Birth details stay in the app's storage and can be deleted in one tap. Palm photos are analysed in memory and never stored.

LazyOracle is a companion for reflection and entertainment. It is not advice about health, money, law or safety and does not predict events.
