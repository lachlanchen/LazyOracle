LazyOracle Support

LazyOracle is made by LazyingArt LLC. Charts are computed on your device, with cloud narration available through Tianji Cloud. There is no account to recover.

Common questions

How do I explore the Change Atlas?

Open Change Atlas from the native app's home screen, choose a hexagram and tap individual lines. Compare the before and after figures, inspect the trigrams, or open a related transformation. Reset clears the changed lines. This works offline.

How do I keep a result and revisit it?

Tap Save to notebook under a calculated result. Open Reading notebook from home, select the entry, add a reflection, a small action or a later observation, and tap Save notes before leaving. The original calculation stays unchanged. You can mark entries reviewed, filter pending entries and export a text copy through the share sheet. Entries are local to the device; no account or automatic sync is provided.

How do I get a plain-language explanation?

In a native practice, use Explain this reading or enter a follow-up question in the bottom composer. Tianji needs an internet connection and receives the selected result and your question. It does not recast the result. If unavailable, the calculation remains on screen; retry later. No language-model download is needed. The classic web interface additionally has a Tianji Cloud switch in Settings.

How does a detailed BaZi report work?

Enter your birth details and view the free summary first. The optional detailed report costs USD4.99 per report (native stores show the local equivalent). Each purchase is for one saved chart snapshot and question, not a subscription or a permanent upgrade. Reports cover the chart, five-element balance, work, relationships, luck cycles, the current year and practical reflection.

If payment is pending, wait for the store's confirmation. If the analysis is interrupted, open the same report and retry without paying again. Use Recover unfinished purchases if the store completed payment but delivery was interrupted. Web checkout is verified by the server; returning from Stripe alone does not unlock a report. Keep app/browser data, and export completed reports before removing it. Purchases and report access do not automatically move between devices or stores.

For payment or delivery help, email the support address below with the report ID and store order reference; do not send payment-card details. Apple/Google purchases and Stripe checkout receipts provide their respective refund/support options.

The compass does not move.

Allow the requested sensor permission, hold the phone flat and keep it away from magnets. In the classic browser interface, tap "Enable the compass" first. Some tablets and desktop browsers have no compass; enter a heading instead.

The face or hand view cannot measure.

Allow camera access in the device settings. Use even light, keep the face or one open palm fully in frame, and hold still while stable frames are collected. For a hand, spread your fingers slightly. Live measurement needs a physical camera and is unavailable in a simulator.

My birth time is unknown.

Tick "I do not know the birth time". The hour pillar and the Ascendant are then approximate and the app says so.

Where is my data saved?

Profiles, saved practice state, conversations and notebook entries are stored locally. Settings lets you edit your birth profile. Removing local app data also removes the notebook, so export any entries you want to keep first. The classic web interface has a separate birth-details deletion control.
